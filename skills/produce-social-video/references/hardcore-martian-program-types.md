# Hardcore Martian program types

Classify every `硬核火星人` production on two independent axes before writing the brief.

## Axis 1: program type

### `member_original`

Use for paid/member episodes in the approved `马斯克原典` curriculum.

- Follow the approved curriculum topic and sequence; do not branch into a generic Musk topic.
- Deliver primary-source close reading, method explanation, boundaries, and a transferable exercise or conclusion.
- Do not promote membership, tease another member episode, or turn the lesson into a lead-generation piece.
- Require no fatal gate, score `>=9.5`, and a completed full linear watch.
- Read and apply [hardcore-martian-member-standard.md](hardcore-martian-member-standard.md).

### `public_story`

Use for a free public-facing story, event, profile, prologue, or audience-entry video.

- Optimize for cold-viewer comprehension, curiosity, story movement, evidence, and a shareable conclusion.
- A membership bridge or call to action is allowed only when the approved brief explicitly requires it. Otherwise omit it.
- Never reveal or pre-announce unavailable member content.
- For an official `硬核火星人` public release, target no fatal gate and score `>=9.5`; generic public videos outside the series may use the ordinary `>=9.0` threshold.

### `custom`

Use only when neither house type fits. Record the reason and confirm the differing identity and release rules.

## Axis 2: edit model

### `source_led`

Use when one authoritative event/interview/source supplies the story and visual continuity, as in `trump-granddaughter-elon-starship`.

- Let original full-screen footage dominate.
- Alternate concise Chinese context with verified original-audio evidence.
- Use normal subtitles and a light watermark; avoid knowledge cards, decorative B-roll, and large persistent overlays.
- Preserve valuable pauses, reactions, room tone, and cause/effect inside the source event.

### `narration_led_montage`

Use when Chinese narration owns the story spine and multiple real sources provide evidence/emotion, as in `musk-success-code-prologue`.

- Build a causal documentary montage around narration.
- Use real high-definition footage for roughly 70–80% of the viewing time; reserve designed explanation for structure, numbers, or methods.
- Every shot must map to the current sentence; do not use generic technology filler.
- Use original audio only when the exact quotation adds authority or character and can be verified.

### `hybrid_evidence_lesson`

Default for `member_original` when the lesson alternates Chinese explanation, primary-source excerpts, cases, and restrained diagrams.

- Target Musk/source original audio around 45–50% when sufficient authoritative material exists.
- Target real footage around 60% and designed explanation around 40%; source-evidence passages use full-screen source by default.
- Use graphics to clarify mechanisms, calculations, boundaries, or transfer exercises, never as a continuous PPT replacement.
- Remove the explanation canvas when returning to source footage; do not embed the interview inside the preceding slide system.

## Routing examples

| Project | program_type | edit_model | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `musk-first-principles-02-theoretical-lower-bound` | `member_original` | `hybrid_evidence_lesson` | Curriculum lesson and primary-source method analysis |
| `trump-granddaughter-elon-starship` | `public_story` | `source_led` | One rare source event supplies the full story |
| `musk-success-code-prologue` | `public_story` | `narration_led_montage` | Narrated crisis-to-method story built from many sources |

Program type takes precedence over surface appearance. A member lesson with full-screen interview footage remains `member_original`; a free story that explains a method remains `public_story`.
