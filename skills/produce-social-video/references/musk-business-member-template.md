# 马斯克商业解读会员视频模板

Use this template for `硬核火星人 / 马斯克商业解读` paid episodes. It adapts the transferable strengths of high-performing Chinese business explainers without copying a creator's catchphrases, on-camera persona, thumbnails, or ornamental editing.

## Program contract

- `program_type`: `member_original`
- Default `edit_model`: `narration_led_montage`; use `hybrid_evidence_lesson` when primary-source audio materially advances the argument.
- House default duration, updated by the user on 2026-09-09: approximately 5 minutes including the fixed outro, superseding 10–12 minutes. Read [five-minute-focus-and-recall.md](five-minute-focus-and-recall.md) and lock scope before TTS. An opening sample uses the approved opening without compressing the whole argument into it.
- Premium target: `>=9.8/10`, zero fatal gates, independent rescore, and a completed normal-speed linear watch.
- Paid value must come from accounting reconstruction, unit economics, incentives, capital allocation, control, scenario analysis, or falsifiable predictions—not public facts rearranged into a list. Choose the mechanism needed for this episode; these are alternatives, not a mandatory multi-topic checklist.

## One commercial mystery, one explanation engine

Write the episode around one contradiction with money, power, time, or risk at stake. Before scripting, fill:

```text
Visible result / familiar belief:
Contradictory fact:
Who chooses and who pays:
Single minimal model:
What the model explains:
Where it fails:
Viewer's usable decision rule:
```

Every chapter must test or extend that same model. Facts that do not change the model, raise a new obstacle, expose a cost, or answer an open question are research notes, not script material.

## Narrative spine

1. **Cold open—visible contradiction (0–15s):** recognizable subject, concrete number/action, and a question with a cost. Start paying off the promise with one useful answer rather than withholding everything.
2. **Promise and first model (15–45s):** translate the puzzle into one plain-language relationship the viewer can repeat.
3. **Build the case:** show the first decisive evidence and explain what it proves—not merely what it says.
4. **Upgrade the question:** reveal why the first answer is incomplete; introduce the next constraint, payer, opponent, or accounting boundary.
5. **Mechanism in motion:** use a worked example, money flow, capacity chain, or scenario to make the model operational.
6. **Counterforce and limit:** present the strongest competing explanation, failure condition, or unknown. Do not manufacture a villain.
7. **Our judgment:** distinguish verified fact, source claim, calculation, and `硬核火星人` interpretation. State what would falsify the interpretation.
8. **Resolution:** answer the opening mystery with one memorable decision rule and explain what evidence should be watched next.

Maintain a question-escalation chain: each answer closes one loop and opens a more consequential one. Do not label beats as “反转” in narration; let evidence change the viewer's model.

## The Xiaolin-transfer test

The transferable engine is:

`familiar surface → conflicting fact → repeatable minimal model → escalating cases → incentives/choices → cost or limit → usable framework`

Use ordinary-language analogies, decision-makers with different incentives, documents/data/visible actions as proof, lightness after complexity, and a new information reward every 15–30 seconds.

Do not copy signature phrases, presenter mannerisms, thumbnail composition, comedic identity, unsupported crisis language, or data density used as a substitute for causal explanation.

## Visual grammar

- Target about 60% full-screen real footage/evidence and 40% programmed explanation; vary by evidence availability, not by a mechanical timer.
- Organize the body into coherent 20–40 second scene groups: establishing state → action → change → result.
- Assign every shot one primary job: evidence, explanation, emotion, or seam.
- Use real footage for place, person, process, consequence, and emotion. Use graphics only for relationships footage cannot show: flows, comparisons, time, scenarios, ownership, accounting boundaries, and causal chains.
- Never run the full episode as slides. Do not place interviews or launch footage inside decorative white cards.
- Preserve source geometry. Reject generic rocket/factory/keyboard filler even when visually attractive.

## 98-point gates

A technically clean video is insufficient. To qualify for `>=9.8`:

- first frame identifies the subject; first 3 seconds show a conflict, abnormal consequence, or costly choice;
- first 15 seconds deliver both the promise and a useful partial answer;
- no 30-second interval lacks a new fact, mechanism, correction, example, decision rule, or purposeful evidence-driven dramatic hold;
- every major claim is visibly bound to evidence or explicitly labeled analysis;
- at least one serious counterargument or failure condition modifies the thesis;
- a viewer can restate the model and apply it to a real company or decision;
- motion graphics clarify relationships and remain subordinate to the narrative;
- all ordinary member-video technical, voice, caption, picture, brand, provenance, and full-watch gates pass.
