# Creator learning and optimization loop

Use this loop when the user asks CutCircuit to study a creator, deepen its own method, or optimize toward repeated high-quality output.

## Evidence layers

Keep four layers separate:

1. **Observable:** public title, thumbnail, transcript, chapters, shot/sound change, published date, and public engagement snapshot.
2. **Derived:** narrative beats, explanation model, visual function, rhetorical move, and measurable edit density.
3. **Hypothesis:** why a choice may help comprehension, retention, trust, or sharing.
4. **Confirmed house rule:** supported by multiple samples and/or the channel's own post-publish results.

Public view counts do not establish retention causality. Never present an inferred mechanism as algorithm fact.

## Study loop

1. Build a reproducible sample set: recent, most-viewed, topic-matched, and at least one counterexample.
2. Archive URLs, dates, duration, public metrics, subtitles/chapters, and evidence gaps.
3. Annotate packaging, opening, question chain, information rewards, model, cases, counterforce, resolution, visual functions, and audio punctuation.
4. Extract mechanisms, not identity. Record what not to copy.
5. Convert each promising mechanism into a falsifiable production experiment changing only one or two variables.
6. Pre-register success evidence: first-frame clarity, 3/15/30-second promise delivery, viewer-value audit, comment restatement, saves/shares, or platform retention when available.
7. After publication, review at 24 hours and 7 days against the channel's recent median, while noting sample size and distribution changes.
8. Promote a rule into CutCircuit only after repeated evidence; otherwise keep it as a dated hypothesis in the project ledger.
