## 1. Evidence levels

Label every recommendation:

- **Officially confirmed:** published by WeChat/Tencent or an authoritative regulator quoting the platform.
- **Reported platform guidance:** a credible report from a WeChat creator event, but not a public technical specification.
- **Working heuristic:** a testable editorial practice inferred from product surfaces, analytics, or repeated creator results.

Never convert a working heuristic into a claimed algorithm weight. Exact traffic-pool sizes, interaction weights, pass lines, and formulas are unsafe unless current official documentation states them.

## 2. Distribution model

Official disclosure supports a dual mechanism:

1. **Friend recommendation:** WeChat uses social aggregation to show content friends recommend. Content recommended by more friends may receive higher exposure ordering and friend-recommendation prompts.
2. **Platform algorithm recommendation:** personalized recommendation supplies content beyond the immediate social graph; users can adjust or disable personalization and give negative feedback.

Operational implication: optimize for both **viewer satisfaction** and **public endorsement**. A private like on another platform and a visible friend recommendation in WeChat do not carry the same social cost. Do not ask only “will people watch?” Ask “will they want their acquaintances to know they endorse this?”

Content quality and compliance remain gates. Friend activity is not permission to use misleading, copied, low-quality, or unsafe content.

## 3. Two encounter surfaces

Design two complete paths.

### Autoplay encounter

`first visible frame → first spoken/subtitled line → early payoff → continued watch`

- Do not rely on a thumbnail click to create the first impression.
- Put the recognizable person/object and the unusual fact in the first beat.
- Make subtitles useful for muted or noisy environments.
- Remove greetings, channel slogans, generic biography, and “今天聊聊”.

### Forwarded-card encounter

`sender's endorsement → card/title/cover → recipient opens → promise is confirmed`

- The card must make sense outside the creator's profile and outside the original conversation.
- A recipient should know who/what it concerns, what is unusual, and why the sender considered it worth sharing.
- Verify the actual current chat, group, Moments, profile, and search previews before an important release; crops and displayed text can change.

The two paths share one promise but not necessarily one hook. The feed can open with motion or a startling line; the forwarded card can use a calmer, more endorsable title.

## 4. Title and publishing copy

### MuskKnowledgeLab short-title hard gate

For this workspace, every WeChat Channels short title must contain **fewer than 16 Chinese characters**. Count and display the character count beside every recommended short title before declaring a package ready. This is a delivery constraint, not an editorial preference: a natural long title may be useful as a description lead or a forwarding sentence, but it must not be supplied as the video title. Move the mechanism, qualifiers, and social-forwarding context into the cover, publishing-copy first line, and opening beat.

Prefer a natural sentence a real person could repeat while forwarding:

- recognizable subject + unexpected action + consequential question;
- concrete number/scale + human interpretation;
- familiar explanation + deeper correction;
- event + “why this matters beyond the event”.

For the “硬核火星人” account, favor titles that let a viewer pass along a new mental model, not merely spectacle. Example shape:

`马斯克押上[有来源的数字]造芯片厂，他真正担心的可能不是芯片短缺`

Rules:

- Use conversational Chinese and a complete thought within the short-title limit; use the publishing-copy first line and forwarding sentence when additional context is needed.
- Front-load recognizable entities and stakes. Put unfamiliar project names later.
- Use one main information gap. Two unrelated questions dilute the forwarding message.
- Preserve uncertainty words such as “计划”“可能”“据其表示” when required.
- Avoid press-release syntax, stacked nouns, empty superlatives, fake urgency, and titles that make the sharer look gullible.
- Use a question mark only when the audience cares about a non-obvious answer. After raising the question, soon provide a concrete clue, action, or conflict relevant to it. The conclusion may come later, provided the buildup keeps increasing understanding or anticipation and the video ultimately delivers its title promise. Do not require an immediate answer or premature declaration of the author’s position.
- Do not paste the cover's short phrase into the publishing copy verbatim.

Produce at least two materially different variants based on different forwarding motives:

1. **knowledge/status:** “这解释了一个很多人没看见的产业变化。”
2. **future/identity:** “这代表一种我愿意认同的未来判断。”
3. **care/usefulness:** “这对某个具体的人有用，我想发给他。”
4. **conversation:** “这件事值得我们讨论。”

Select the motive that matches the account and content; do not force emotional warmth onto hard-tech analysis.

## 5. Cover and first frame

Treat cover and first frame as separate assets even if they use the same source image.

### Cover

- Optimize first for the forwarded card, profile grid, search, and secondary discovery surfaces.
- Use one recognizable subject, one scale/mechanism cue, and short copy.
- Favor legible human/object scale over decorative sci-fi complexity.
- Keep the copy socially endorsable and factually bounded.
- Recompose 3:4 and 4:3; inspect actual WeChat previews. Keep a 16:9 master for cross-platform use, but do not assume it is the primary 视频号 display.

### First frame

- Optimize for autoplay: bright, clear, immediate subject recognition, and visible motion or visual tension.
- Do not begin on a logo, black frame, slow fade, distant establishing shot, or unreadable render.
- If the strongest fact is numerical, pair the number with a physical image or comparison immediately.
- Burn or overlay a concise first-line subtitle when needed; it must remain inside UI-safe zones.

## 6. Script and social value

Use the general `hook → surprise → reversal → elevation` structure, but make the elevation socially transferable.

Use 15–30 seconds as a pacing diagnostic, not a mechanical answer deadline. Reward attention through a developing scene, action, or consequential choice, or through at least one of:

- a new verified fact;
- a visualizable comparison;
- a mechanism explanation;
- a correction of the viewer's likely assumption;
- a consequence the viewer can retell.

Before the ending, formulate the **forwardable sentence**: the one idea a viewer could repeat to a colleague or friend. Earn it through evidence before stating it. For “硬核火星人”, connect present engineering to a civilization-scale future through an explicit causal bridge; label account interpretation separately from official project purpose.

Do not mistake generic inspiration for shareability. “未来已来” is weak; a concrete causal insight that changes how someone sees the event is strong.

## 7. Description, topics, and CTA

- Write the first line as useful context, not a second sensational title.
- Add the central takeaway and necessary attribution/boundary in compact form.
- Use only topic terms that classify the actual content. Avoid a tag wall.
- A discussion prompt should invite a real judgment, not “你怎么看”.
- For public-facing 硬核火星人 videos, design one real disagreement or decision tradeoff and formulate the closing prompt around the criterion that separates the two sides. Where relevant, represent both Musk supporters' strongest case and skeptics' strongest case before asking for judgment.
- Conflict must come from verified facts, attributed positions, feasibility, cost, timing, risk, or values. Do not invent opponents, distort either side, or conceal a known answer to bait corrections.
- Prefer a natural social action: “如果你身边有人也在关注算力瓶颈，这条值得和他讨论。” Use sparingly and only when it fits.
- Avoid coercive or transactional engagement bait, fake benefits, forced sharing, and repeated “点赞转发关注” commands.

For research-heavy technology videos, keep a clean audience-facing description and a concise source note. If the interface does not present source links well, retain the evidence ledger in the project package even when the public copy must be short.

## 8. Measurement and iteration

Use the 视频号 funnel:

`exposure → early hold → average watch/finish → recommend/forward → recipient opens/watches → follow or meaningful interaction`

Reported 2025 creator-event guidance highlighted early playback, completion, average play duration, forwarding, likes, and comments, while also noting that a forward with poor downstream opening is not inherently valuable. Treat this as reported guidance, not a published weight formula.

Diagnose:

| Symptom | Likely problem | First revision |
|---|---|---|
| weak early hold | first frame/line is vague or slow | show entity + abnormal fact immediately |
| good early hold, poor average watch | buildup lacks meaningful clues, action, or development; body repeats | strengthen story progression and relevant evidence; cut empty transitions without automatically moving the conclusion forward |
| good watch, few friend recommendations | informative but socially costly or not retellable | strengthen forwardable insight and endorsement-safe framing |
| many forwards, weak downstream watch | sender CTA worked but card/promise or recipient payoff failed | redesign forwarded card and first recipient payoff |
| good viewing, weak follow | one-off fact with no account-level future value | make recurring expertise and next question clear |
| search/profile discovery weak | entity or topic is hidden | clarify title/publishing copy and classification terms |

Log variants by title-cover-first-line combination. Do not infer causality from simultaneous changes or one successful post. Prefer repeated patterns across comparable topics and audience cohorts.

## 9. Myths to reject

Reject unsupported claims such as:

- “朋友点赞固定占 55%”；
- “完播超过 45% 自动进入万人池”；
- fixed 300/500/1000-person traffic pools;
- exact ranking multipliers for likes, comments, favorites, or forwards;
- “posting at one magic time guarantees distribution”；
- purchased likes, mutual-engagement groups, or forced forwarding as legitimate optimization.

These numbers circulate widely but are not established by current public official documentation. They can also produce the wrong objective: synthetic interaction rather than satisfied viewers and valuable downstream viewing.
