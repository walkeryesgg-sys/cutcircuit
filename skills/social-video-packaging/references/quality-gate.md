# Unified package quality gate

Use this gate before recommending any final title, description, script, or cover system. Score the actual package, not the intention behind it.

## Fatal gates

Reject and revise if any answer is yes:

- Does title, cover, or narration upgrade a plan, render, estimate, attribution, or possibility into fact?
- Does the package promise an answer or visual proof the video does not deliver?
- Can generated or composited imagery be mistaken for documentary evidence?
- Are title/cover/body materially unrelated, or is trend bait borrowed from an unrelated event?
- Does the opening evade the advertised subject, or does the buildup fail to supply relevant clues, action, conflict, or increasing understanding? Delaying the final answer is acceptable when the story meaningfully progresses and ultimately fulfills the promise.
- For a public-facing 硬核火星人 script, do the first three seconds lack a familiar entity plus a concrete abnormal fact/conflict and an answerable information gap?
- Is the required account-exclusive analysis absent, merely a summary, or unsupported by a stated factual bridge?
- Is the supposed disagreement fabricated, straw-manned, based on a false premise, or engineered by withholding a settled fact?
- Is the cover unreadable or unrecognizable at feed-card size?
- Does the package violate known copyright, platform, safety, or disclosure requirements?

## Score out of 100

| Dimension | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence and promise integrity | 20 | every major claim is supported, attributed, bounded, and delivered |
| Title | 15 | clear entity/stake, real tension, natural language, platform fit |
| Cover | 15 | one focal subject, one tension, mobile legibility, truthful visual proof |
| Opening and body retention | 20 | first-three-second hard hook, early meaningful clues/action/conflict, progressive story and information rewards, earned ending |
| Platform-native adaptation | 10 | copy and ratios suit the actual discovery surface rather than being pasted across platforms |
| Description, search, and credibility | 10 | truncated first lines retain value; entities are natural; sources/boundaries are useful |
| Technical and accessibility | 5 | correct exports, safe zones, readable contrast, captions/credits where relevant |
| Testability | 5 | variants change a meaningful thesis; hypothesis and qualified metric are named |

For public-facing 硬核火星人 work, the opening/body score also requires an evidence-backed account-exclusive analysis and a genuine disagreement or decision tradeoff. Missing either caps this dimension at 10/20 even before applying the fatal gates.

Interpretation:

- 92–100: strong release candidate, assuming no fatal gate.
- 85–91: publishable after listed minor revisions.
- 75–84: promising but packaging or retention has a material weakness.
- below 75: rebuild the lead promise or execution.

## Evidence rule for scoring

For every score, quote or point to the exact title words, cover element, opening line, description passage, or test design that earns the points. “Feels strong” earns nothing. List the three highest-impact revisions in priority order.

## Mobile acceptance test

The package passes only if a cold viewer can answer within roughly one glance:

1. What or who is this about?
2. What is unusual or consequential?
3. Why should I choose it now?

The cover need not answer all three alone; title and cover must answer them together. The opening must then confirm that expectation without restarting from generic background.

## WeChat Channels addendum

When 视频号 is primary, add five checks:

1. **Autoplay:** does the first visible frame and first spoken/subtitled line stop a viewer without relying on the cover?
2. **Endorsement safety:** would a viewer feel comfortable letting acquaintances see that they recommended this?
3. **Forwarded-card clarity:** can a recipient understand the subject and reason to open without knowing the sender's context?
4. **Recipient payoff:** does the forwarded recipient receive the promised value quickly, rather than only the original follower?
5. **Social value:** does the video give the viewer useful knowledge, identity expression, emotional connection, care for others, or conversation currency worth passing on?

For a 视频号-first package, a failure on autoplay or forwarded-card clarity caps the package below 85 even if the generic score is higher.
