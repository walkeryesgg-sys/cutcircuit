# End-of-video homepage CTA rotation

Use this module for public-facing 硬核火星人 narration. Its goal is to move an interested viewer into the profile to watch more videos, not merely collect likes or follows.

## Selection rules

1. Select exactly one eligible CTA at random for each script.
2. When recent outputs are available, exclude the CTA used in the previous three videos before selecting. Never use the same CTA in consecutive videos.
3. Adapt only the bracketed topic words. Preserve the natural spoken rhythm; do not stack a second “点赞、关注、转发” command.
4. Integrate the CTA into the final 1–2 sentences after the video's earned conclusion. Make it sound like the next chapter of the same worldview.
5. Keep the final CTA to roughly 20–45 Chinese characters when possible. Remove it if it creates a tonal break, repeats the preceding sentence, or makes the ending feel like an advertisement.
6. Skip by default for `member_original`, solemn interview/source excerpts, memorial or tragedy content, and any explicit no-CTA request.
7. The preferred action is “进入/点进主页继续看”. Mention following only as a secondary action when it sounds natural.

## Topic-sensitive choice

- Prefer A, C, or G for broad public stories and new viewers.
- Prefer B or D for analysis, business, engineering, and first-principles topics.
- Prefer E for spaceflight, Mars, civilization, and future-facing endings.
- Prefer F for fast-paced news, short videos, and strong serial-content momentum.

Randomize within the eligible group. Topic fit is a gate; randomness chooses among the versions that fit.

## Reject these patterns

- “点赞关注不迷路” and other generic creator clichés.
- Three or more actions in one sentence, such as like + comment + follow + share + profile.
- False scarcity, fake benefits, giveaways, or promises that the profile cannot fulfill.
- A sudden brand slogan after a solemn or emotionally complete final line.
- Repeating the exact title, cover hook, or opening promise in the CTA.
