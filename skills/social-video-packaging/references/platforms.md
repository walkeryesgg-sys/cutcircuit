## 1. Shared rules

All major recommendation systems ultimately respond to viewer choice and satisfaction, though surfaces differ. Package for the discovery surface:

- search needs explicit entities and query language;
- recommendation needs immediate relevance and curiosity;
- social sharing needs a title the sharer is comfortable endorsing;
- subscribers tolerate more context and recurring formats than cold audiences.

Do not copy one platform's slang, punctuation, hashtag volume, or description structure to all others.

Rule confidence labels:

- **Officially confirmed:** current platform documentation supports the claim.
- **Stable editorial tendency:** repeated creator practice and surface design support it, but it is not a disclosed algorithm weight.
- **Verify at publish:** limits, UI crops, test availability, and moderation rules can change.

## 2. WeChat Channels（视频号）

Treat WeChat Channels as a distinct social-distribution product, not a Douyin copy. Official disclosure confirms a combination of friend recommendation and platform algorithm recommendation, and says content recommended by more friends may receive higher exposure ordering. Read [wechat-channels.md](wechat-channels.md) for the complete workflow, evidence levels, title/copy patterns, cover and first-frame rules, and analytics loop.

Default package: one forwarding-safe natural title/publishing copy, one alternate with a different social motive, restrained topic terms, a 3:4-first cover plus 4:3 fallback, the exact first-frame/first-line plan, and a forwarding-context audit.

## 3. Douyin（抖音）

Douyin's official creator education emphasizes that a quality cover should reveal the content keyword or core information at a glance, use a clear and harmonious image, and keep composition/background readable. The fast-feed principles below are stable editorial guidance. TikTok's official creative guidance also supports 9:16, high-resolution, UI-safe, sound-on, hook-body-close construction and an early hook; treat that as transferable short-form craft, not proof of Douyin's organic ranking formula.

Optimize for immediate comprehension in a fast vertical feed and strong early retention.

- Put the recognizable entity, tension, or surprising consequence in the first clause.
- Use spoken Chinese. Remove setup phrases and institutional language.
- Questions work only when the first seconds visibly engage the question.
- Treat caption text as context/classification, not a second script.
- Use a few precise hashtags: entity, vertical/topic, and series/community where relevant.
- Avoid unsupported “全网第一”“彻底颠覆”“行业完了” claims.
- For technical content, translate one abstract number into a visual comparison.

Default package: a short spoken-style title/caption lead, a small set of precise hashtags, a 3:4 cover designed to crop safely in vertical UI, and a first-3-second line.

## 4. Bilibili（B站）

These are stable community/editorial tendencies. Verify current submission fields and moderation rules in the creator center; do not claim an exact recommendation weight without official evidence.

Optimize for topic recognition, search, community expectations, and long-form value.

- Combine searchable entities with a clear analytical promise.
- Longer explanatory titles can work, but front-load the subject and core tension.
- Use brackets only for genuine format labels such as 原声、双语、实测、纪录片; avoid decorative bracket spam.
- The description may include thesis, key questions, chapters, sources, corrections, production credits, and copyright context.
- Choose accurate partitions and tags; expert communities punish vague sensationalism.
- Cover text may be conceptual if the publishing title carries the searchable facts.

Default package: one search-balanced title, one curiosity-led alternate, structured description, chapters for long videos, source list, relevant tags, and 16:9-first cover.

## 5. Xiaohongshu（小红书）

Official platform guidance requires the cover/title to connect truthfully to the body and treats unrelated hot-topic bait as misleading. The style guidance below is a stable editorial tendency, not a disclosed ranking formula.

Optimize for search intent, saving, personal usefulness, and note-like authenticity.

- Frame hard topics through a human question, takeaway, comparison, or “看懂” value.
- Use concise, conversational titles with concrete nouns. Avoid fake diary voice when the account is analytical.
- Structure the description for scanning: opening conclusion, 3–6 takeaways, boundary/source note, and one discussion prompt.
- Include natural search terms in sentences as well as selective hashtags.
- Make the cover legible as a small card; one dominant subject and a short benefit/question usually outperform a miniature poster.
- Avoid excessive emojis and formulaic “家人们”“一定要看” unless native to the account.

Default package: one search/usefulness title, one curiosity title, scannable note copy, a restrained set of relevant topics, and 3:4-first cover.

## 6. YouTube

Optimize separately for Home/Suggested and Search.

- Decide whether the lead strategy is searchable or intriguing; hybridize only when clarity remains.
- Be accurate and succinct; front-load important words because titles may truncate.
- Use the title and thumbnail together to communicate value and intrigue rather than duplicate text.
- The opening must immediately reassure viewers that the title/thumbnail promise will be delivered.
- Put the essential context and value in the first two description lines. Follow with chapters, primary sources, credits, links, and a focused CTA as relevant.
- Tags are secondary; use them mainly for genuine variants and misspellings, not stuffing.
- For long-form videos, use YouTube's native title/thumbnail testing when available and judge the winner by qualified watch time, not CTR alone.

These principles reflect official YouTube guidance on appeal, engagement, satisfaction, accurate concise titles, metadata relevance, and native A/B tests.
