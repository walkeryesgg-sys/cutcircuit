## Discovery routes

Run complementary routes:

1. Formal formats: interview, conversation, podcast, keynote, presentation, Q&A.
2. Field access: tour, walkthrough, inside, behind the scenes, factory, launch site, shop floor.
3. Natural interaction: talking with, discusses, answers, candid, informal, one-on-one.
4. Topic: company, product, engineering system, event, year.
5. Creator expansion: scan uploads from channels that produced at least one verified high-quality original.
6. Seed expansion: use user-approved URLs as recall tests and sources of channel/event vocabulary.

Do not require a format word in the title. Use format words for recall and scoring only.

## Quality score (100)

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Musk/target speaker participates continuously | 20 |
| Original interviewer, host, company, or event source | 15 |
| Complete context and limited editing | 15 |
| Viewpoint or Q&A information density | 15 |
| Rare field access or engineering environment | 10 |
| Natural interaction and human texture | 10 |
| Captions available and usable | 5 |
| At least 720p; 4K earns full points | 5 |
| Creator has verified original-source history | 5 |

Penalties: repost −20; commentary dominates −20; short-context edit −15; target speaker share low −15; sensational title −10.

Metadata alone may assign only a provisional score. Promote to verified quality after caption or human review.

## Canonical source choice

Prefer, in order:

1. original creator/company/event upload;
2. most complete continuous version;
3. highest usable resolution and audio;
4. best captions and stable URL;
5. earliest upload when other signals tie.

Retain mirrors as fallback URLs. Keep clips linked to their parent event.

## Golden-set evaluation

Maintain user-approved seeds and obvious negative examples. Each rule change must report:

- golden recall;
- negative rejection rate;
- new candidate volume;
- top rejection reasons;
- historical backfill coverage.

Do not accept a change that recalls seeds only by flooding the archive with commentary and reposts.
